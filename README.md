Why Analyze Sales Data?
Customer Behavior

Modern enterprises generate huge volumes of daily transactions but often fail to understand customer buying behavior, customer segments, and churn factors without structured analysis.

Sales Volatility

Failure to identify seasonal trends, product life cycles, and promotional impacts can lead to stock shortages or excess inventory.

Manual Decisions

Making business decisions based on intuition rather than analytics reduces overall ROI and business efficiency.

Solution

IBM SPSS Modeler transforms raw transactional data into actionable business insights.
